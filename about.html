Our Story

From Campus LANs to Championship Stages

BCE Esports started with a simple idea — students who love gaming deserve a platform as serious and professional as any sport on campus. What began as informal LAN sessions between hostel rooms has grown into an organised competitive ecosystem.

Today, BCE Esports brings together players, teams, and fans under a single identity. We organise tournaments across multiple titles — from battle royale giants like BGMI and Free Fire to tactical shooters like Valorant and COD Mobile, and even strategy titles like Chess.

Our goal is bigger than winning matches. We are building a lasting esports culture at BCE Bihar — one that respects competitive integrity, celebrates teamwork, and gives every student a place to grow as a player, organiser, or fan.

Our Mission

To create a professional, fair, and inclusive competitive gaming environment for every BCE student — one where talent is discovered, skills are sharpened, and every match is played with integrity and passion.

🚀

Our Vision
