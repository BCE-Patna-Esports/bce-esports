About This Tournament

BCE Winter Clash 2026 is the flagship BGMI tournament of BCE Esports, open to all currently enrolled students of Bakhtiyarpur College of Engineering. Teams will battle across multiple rounds in the classic battle royale format, competing for the championship title and a share of the ₹20,000 prize pool.

The tournament runs across three days with group stages followed by semifinals and a grand final. Each match awards placement points and kill points, with the top teams from each group advancing to the next round.

All matches will be streamed live on our official YouTube channel. Teams must complete their registration before the closing date and ensure their roster meets all eligibility criteria.

Eligibility & Rules

  • Eligibility: All players must be currently enrolled BCE students with a valid college ID.
  • Email verification: Captain must register with a valid @bcebakhtiyarpur.ac.in email.
  • Roster: Exactly 4 main players + 1 optional substitute. Substitutes must also meet eligibility.
  • Team limit: A player may not be part of two teams in this tournament.
  • Fair play: Any use of hacks, scripts, emulators, or teaming is an immediate disqualification.
  • Reporting: Players must join the match lobby at least 10 minutes before the scheduled start time.
